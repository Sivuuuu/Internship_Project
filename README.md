## 📌 Projects Overview

### 01. Warehouse Capacity & Optimization Model
A capacity planning model designed to evaluate warehouse requirements over a multi-year period[cite: 1]. The model considers inventory growth, peak demand, warehouse capacity, and safety margins to identify potential capacity requirements and risks[cite: 1].

* **Key Focus Areas:**
  * Business assumptions and input modeling[cite: 1]
  * Inventory growth analysis[cite: 1]
  * Capacity requirement calculations[cite: 1]
  * Peak inventory and demand analysis[cite: 1]
  * Safe capacity assessment[cite: 1]
  * Multi-year capacity planning[cite: 1]
  * Risk identification[cite: 1]
* **Tools Used:** Google Sheets[cite: 1]

### 02. Monte Carlo Cricket Simulation
A probability-based simulation model developed to study possible cricket match outcomes[cite: 1]. The model uses random events and probability distributions to simulate match scenarios and analyze outcomes such as runs, wickets, team performance, and winning probability[cite: 1].

* **Key Focus Areas:**
  * Probability-based modeling[cite: 1]
  * Random event generation[cite: 1]
  * Match-event simulation[cite: 1]
  * Runs and wickets analysis[cite: 1]
  * Team performance metrics[cite: 1]
  * Winning probability estimation[cite: 1]
  * Simulation-based analysis[cite: 1]
* **Tools Used:** Google Sheets[cite: 1]

### 03. Fantasy Cricket Player Optimization
A player analytics and optimization model focused on selecting a fantasy cricket team based on player performance and defined selection constraints[cite: 1]. The project involved analyzing player statistics, calculating performance measures, ranking players, and applying optimization techniques to arrive at a suitable team combination[cite: 1].

* **Key Focus Areas:**
  * Player data analysis[cite: 1]
  * Performance metric calculation[cite: 1]
  * Player normalization and ranking[cite: 1]
  * Fantasy point estimation[cite: 1]
  * Player shortlisting[cite: 1]
  * Team composition constraints[cite: 1]
  * Optimization using OpenSolver[cite: 1]
* **Tools Used:** Google Sheets, OpenSolver[cite: 1]
